Getting started

Find your next watch.

  1. Start with the weekly feed or search. Look through reported additions, or search for a title you already have in mind.
  2. Choose the audio you want. “Watch in” checks available audio; “Originally” filters a title’s original language. The two can be different.
  3. Check the offer. A platform may offer a title through a base subscription, add-on, rental or purchase. Follow the provider link to confirm before paying.
  4. Save it for later. The watchlist stays in your current browser. Clearing website storage removes it.

About this space

Built around useful questions.

This is Akash’s independent project space. OTTScout brings streaming discovery into one place, with a focus on the languages people watch in India. The goal is to make the next step clear: find a title, understand its viewing options and open the streaming service.

Clear about the data

OTTScout uses Movie of the Night for streaming availability and TMDB for matched original-language and genre metadata. Uncertain details are marked unverified. It does not host films or sell streaming subscriptions.

Privacy

A simple homepage.

This homepage does not use analytics, advertising scripts, forms or browser storage. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, which processes requests under GitHub’s privacy statement. Following a project or external link takes you to a service with its own privacy practices.

OTTScout has separate browser preferences and optional analytics choices, described in its privacy notice.
